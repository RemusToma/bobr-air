Step 1

Bring it to life

Do this first, before any soldering: it tests the board. You need a laptop and a USB cable that carries data. Pick one of two ways.

You are on a phone. Open this page on a laptop: remustoma.github.io/bobr-air

Option 2 · No code

Install the finished BÓBR AIR

Two minutes in the browser. This is also the way back if your own software goes wrong: it replaces everything on the board.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Safari and Firefox can't talk to USB.
  2. Connect the screen board. Two USB ports? Use the micro-USB port, or a USB-A to USB-C cable.
  3. Click the button for your board, choose the port in the list, wait about a minute.

Steps 2 to 10

Build it

About an hour. Four solder joints and twelve screws. Before you start: the iron gets 350 °C hot, so always put it back in its stand, and open a window. Your new sensor will notice the solder smoke too.

2

Cut and strip the cable

Cut the cable 10 cm from the white plug. You don't need the black plugs.

Strip 3 mm from each wire, twist the strands and tin them with a little solder.

3

Find out which wire is which

Plug the white plug into CN1 on the back of the screen board and leave it there. The pin names are printed next to CN1. Follow each wire and note its colour.

CN1 pinColourSensor pin
3.3Vyour colourVCC
GNDyour colourGND
IO22your colourSCL
IO27your colourSDA
4

Solder the sensor

Push each wire through its hole from the side with the parts. Solder it on the flat side, then cut off what sticks out. SDO and CS stay empty.

Tip: 350 °C with the chisel tip. Heat the pad and the wire for 1 to 2 seconds, then add the solder.

5

Quick test

Connect the board to USB again. The starter now says Sensor OK and shows your first numbers. BÓBR AIR shows the numbers, and air quality says Starting.

Still Not found or No sensor? Check the four joints and your colour table. Then unplug USB.

6

Screen board into the front

Lay the front case face down on a soft towel. Put the board in with the screen down and the USB port at the side opening. The white plug sits in the small pocket under the board.

Fix it with the 4 screws from SCREEN (4 mm) and the small screwdriver. Snug, not tight.

7

Sensor into its own room

Lay the sensor into the T-shaped pocket in the lower room: chip up, pins towards the wire slot. It only fits one way. Fix it with the 2 screws from SENSOR (4 mm), snug, not tight. Lay the wires into the slot.

A small piece of paper tape over the slot keeps the warm air from the board away from the sensor. No Kapton tape and no silicone near the sensor: they can damage it for good.

8

Close the lid

Put the lid on with your name outside. Fix it with the 4 screws from LID (6 mm). Their heads sink into the lid.

9

Base and feet

Stand the case on the base, front edges flush. Fix it from below with the 2 screws from BASE (8 mm).

Then press the 4 rubber feet into the 4 round pockets under the base. They keep it from sliding when you tap the screen.

10

Switch it on

Plug the angled cable into the side, cable pointing back, and into any USB charger.

Board with two USB ports: use a USB-A to USB-C cable and a USB-A charger.

Option 2

Reading the BÓBR AIR screen

Temperature, humidity and pressure are right from the start. Air quality needs time: for the first two days the value moves a lot while the sensor burns in and learns your room. Until it is sure, it says still learning.

  • 0 to 50Good
  • 51 to 100Fine
  • 101 to 150Stale
  • 151 to 200Stuffy. The bóbr: Open a window!
  • over 200Bad. The bóbr: Time for a walk!

If something is wrong

The Install button does nothing, or there is no port in the list

Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop and try another USB cable: many only charge. On Windows the board may need the CH340 USB driver.

The two-port board stays dark on USB-C

Its USB-C port only works with a USB-A to USB-C cable. USB-C to USB-C cables don't work on this board. The micro-USB port works with any data cable.

Claude can't upload to the board

Hold the BOOT button on the board while it says Connecting. Try another USB cable: many only charge. On Windows the board may need the CH340 USB driver.

The screen is white or the colours look strange

The software for the other board type is on it. BÓBR AIR: install again with the other button. Your own software: tell Claude if your board has one or two USB ports.

The picture is upside down

BÓBR AIR: hold a finger on the screen for 3 seconds. It turns by 180 degrees and remembers it. Your own software: ask Claude to turn the picture.

It says No sensor or Not found

Check the four solder joints and compare the colours with your table. It looks for the sensor again every few seconds.

My own software shows nothing

Install BÓBR AIR with option 2. If it works, the hardware is fine and the problem is in the code. Tell Claude what you see.

The temperature looks a bit high

Give it 30 minutes after switching on, keep it out of the sun and away from the laptop's warm air.

Good to know

  • Put it where you sit, away from sun, heaters and open windows. Keep the vents free.
  • No silicone near the sensor. Silicone glue, sealant or Kapton tape can damage it for good.
  • BÓBR AIR saves what it learned every 6 hours, so unplugging is fine.
  • Brain: ESP32. Sensor: Bosch BME680 on I2C, SDA = IO27, SCL = IO22. IO21 is the screen light. All the details are in CLAUDE.md.
